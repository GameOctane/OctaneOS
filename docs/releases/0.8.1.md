## Compatibility

- **Update over the air:** from v0.8.0. The real over-the-air test is still being run; this line is confirmed before the update is announced.
- **Back up first:** yes, as always (keep the device powered during the update).
- **Needs a fresh install:** no.
- **Tested on:** Radxa Cubie A7S. Fresh install of the new build (boots, picture at the display's full size and 120 Hz, RetroAchievements menu and settings, a game starts, scraping without an account).

## What changed

**RetroAchievements pages in the menu work again.** The RetroAchievements page (your points, recent games, progress) and its entry on the home screen used to say "Unauthorized". That page needs a personal **Web API key**, which Batocera's own builds ship inside the program and ours does not. Earning achievements *in a game* was never affected (RetroArch signs in separately). To turn the menu pages on:
1. On retroachievements.org open **Settings > Keys** and copy your **Web API Key** (free, belongs to your account; treat it like a password).
2. On the device: **Game settings > RetroAchievement settings > WEB API KEY**, type it in, and restart the device once.

With several profiles, each profile keeps its own key together with its own RetroAchievements account; switching profile switches the key. Details: `docs/retroachievements.md`.

**Help us find display problems.** The kernel now logs one line at every connection (`OctaneOS 1008: SRC_READY pd_capable=... vbus_present=...`) and there is a new issue form, "No picture, or the wrong picture, on a display", that asks for your cable, your display and those log lines. If your screen stays black or shows the wrong picture, please open one: it takes a minute and tells us which cables behave which way. Nothing about how the picture is chosen changed.

**Scraping (game art and info) is back.** Earlier OctaneOS builds had no ScreenScraper scraping at all; this one does, and it works without an account. You can also enter your own free ScreenScraper account in the scraper settings.

**Settings changed on existing cards:** none.

## Known issues

- **GameCube and Wii games do not start.** The default emulator for both is a standalone Dolphin that is not part of this image, so a game fails at launch. The libretro Dolphin core is in the image, but on this GPU it stalls at start-up (black screen or a frozen logo), so we have not made it the default. Cause is the GPU driver; tracked, no fix yet.
- **USB-C to DisplayPort with a plain (non power-delivery) cable** is tested only with a cable that does negotiate power delivery. If your screen stays black or says "no signal", please open the issue form above.

- After the monitor is switched off and on, or goes to sleep, the picture may need a moment or a replug to come back. It recovered in our test, but we have only repeated it once.
- Some units can switch off completely under heavy load with no heatsink (heat is the main suspect). A heatsink and a fan avoid it in our tests.
- Standalone emulators (PPSSPP, Flycast, DuckStation, standalone mupen64plus) have their own vsync settings and were not tested for this change.

## Files

- `boot.tar.xz` (+ `.md5`, `.sha256`): the over-the-air update. Devices fetch this.
- `boot.tar.xz.manifest` and `boot.tar.xz.manifest.sig`: the signed description of that update (board, version, size, SHA-256). Devices with signature checking refuse an update that does not match.
- `OctaneOS-<build>.img.gz`: a full image for a fresh install.
- `SHA256SUMS`: check downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.
